// Who We Are

An independent
collective.

XrenBeats is a project led by Xren where original music, visual art, and creative people come together. It began as a solo identity and grew into a team of producers, artists, and creators making work from scratch.

Our sound moves through phonk, modern digicore, hyperpop, and whatever comes next. We are not defined by one genre or one format; the project exists to experiment, collaborate, and give independent ideas a real place to live.

Everyone is welcome here. The goal is simple: keep creating, keep sharing, and keep building something genuinely ours.
